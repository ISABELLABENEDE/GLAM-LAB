How Glam Lab works

You already own great outfits you haven't tried yet. Glam Lab helps you find them.

1

Photograph your clothes

Snap a piece on a plain background and upload it. Glam Lab cuts out the background, spots the main colour and names it for you, like "Pink top".

Photo→Cut out
2

Mix or get surprised

Drag pieces onto the outfit board and resize them, or press Surprise me for a fresh combination made only from clothes you own.

Top+Bottom+Shoes
3

Save and plan your week

Keep your favourite looks in the Style Book, give each day of the week an outfit, and tap I wore this to see what you actually wear.

How Glam Lab was made

Glam Lab started as four design boards. I designed every screen myself, then built the working website with Claude, an AI assistant, helping with the code.

1 My design

I planned every page: the pink palette, the fonts, the logo circle, the pill buttons and where every piece of clothing goes.

The clothes on my boards were placeholders showing where items belong. On the live site those boxes stay empty until you upload your own clothes.

2 Coding with Claude

Claude helped me turn the boards into HTML, CSS and JavaScript: the five pages, the drag-and-drop outfit board, the upload flow and the background cut-out.

HTMLCSSJavaScriptCanvasIndexedDBGoogle Fonts

3 Inspiration

The look borrows the playful pink, sparkles and pastel tiles of fashion-doll shops.

The idea of how it works comes from wardrobe apps like GetWardrobe: photograph what you own, get outfit ideas from it, plan your week and track what you wear.
